# UX Vision: The Vedic Skills Platform Experience

## 🧭 Design Philosophy: "Ancient Wisdom, Modern Interaction"
The platform should feel like a **Digital Gurukulam**—reverent and classical in its roots, but agile and personalized in its delivery. It must be accessible to a curious teenager, a busy professional, and a deep-diving scholar simultaneously.

---

## 🎨 Frontend Features for Universal Engagement

### 1. The "Vedic Pulse" (Dynamic Home Feed)
Instead of a static library, users land on a living feed:
- **Daily Sutra**: A hand-picked verse relevant to the user's `LifeStage`.
- **Wisdom Capsules**: Short, 2-minute "Articles" or "Lessons" that apply a specific Vedic principle to a modern scenario (e.g., "Sattva in the Boardroom").
- **Community Highlights**: Recent outreach successes or "Contribution Points" milestones from the community.

### 2. The "Sankalpa" (Goal-Setting) Dashboard
Participation is driven by intention.
- **Set a Sankalpa**: Users choose a goal (e.g., "Master the concepts of Karma Yoga" or "Understand Vedic Domestic Ethics").
- **Adaptive Progress**: The dashboard curates a mini-path from the library to fulfill that specific Sankalpa.
- **Streak & Ritual**: Encourages daily "Study Rituals" rather than binge-reading.

### 3. "Dharma Maps" (Knowledge Visualization)
A visual representation of the Vedic corpus.
- **Progress Heatmap**: Shows which shastras the user has explored.
- **The Four Pillars Chart**: A radar graph showing the user's balance across **Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha** based on their reading and activities.

### 4. Interactive "Bhashya" (Scholar Workspace)
For the deep-divers:
- **Side-by-Side Commentary**: Compare different Sampradayas (e.g., Shankara vs. Ramanuja) in a split-screen view.
- **Linked Highlights**: Click a word in Sanskrit to see its synonyms and usage across *other* shastras in the library.
- **Private Research Folders**: Organize bookmarks into "Projects" or "Articles" the scholar is writing.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized User Panels

### 🛡️ Admin "Command Center"
- **Content Ingestion**: Drag-and-drop JSON/Text files to expand the library.
- **User Analytics**: See which "Learning Curves" are most popular and where users get stuck.
- **Outreach Oversight**: Approve and track "Contribution Points" for community projects.

### 🧘 Mentor "Guru Portal"
- **Student Watchlist**: A dedicated list of assigned students with "Progress Alerts" (e.g., "Student X has been stuck on BG 2.12 for 3 days").
- **Session Scheduler**: Integrated calendar for Guidance Sessions.
- **Doubt Resolver**: A centralized inbox for student questions tagged by library nodes.

### 👥 Student "Shishya Dashboard"
- **My Learning Journey**: Clear view of active Learning Curves.
- **Mentor Feedback**: View notes and advice from their Guru.
- **Badges of Wisdom**: Unlockable titles (e.g., "Gita Jijnasu") as they progress.

---

## 📂 Phase 1.1 UX Focus
Before we build the complex logic, we will implement the **"Vedic Pulse"** and the **"Sankalpa Dashboard"** mocks to allow users to see the *potential* of the platform from day one.
