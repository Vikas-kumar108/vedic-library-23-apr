# Vedic Journey: Sub-Stages of Life & Situations

To fulfill the vision of hyper-personalization, we expand the four core Asramas into a detailed "Journey Map" consisting of 30+ specific life situations.

## 👶 Phase 1: The Formative Years (Shishuh & Bala)
1. **0-5 Years (Matṛ-Guru)**: Emphasis on mother's guidance, foundational habits, and play.
2. **5-10 Years (Pitṛ-Upadesha)**: Transition to father's guidance, introduction to formal discipline and simple dharma.
3. **10-15 Years (Vidyarthi)**: Deepening studies, understanding the goal of life (Bhakti/Devotion).

## 🎓 Phase 2: The Student & Seeker (Brahmacharya)
4. **Early Teenage (Inquisitiveness)**: Dealing with physical and psychological changes, curiosity about the world.
5. **Advanced Studies**: Focus on career (Artha) preparation and psychophysical nature (Swadharma).
6. **Pre-Vocational**: Aligning natural inclinations with societal contribution.

## 💍 Phase 3: The Young Adult & Householder (Grihastha Journey)
7. **The Seeker (Looking for a Partner)**: Pre-marriage guidance, understanding compatibility and ethics.
8. **Newly Married (Vivaha-Prarambha)**: Establishing harmony, setting up a Vedic home.
9. **Young Parent**: Guidance on raising children (0-5, 5-10 stages).
10. **The Provider (Artha-Siddhi)**: Ethical wealth creation, balancing career and family.
11. **Social Pillar**: Engaging in community service and social responsibility.

## 🍃 Phase 4: The Transition (Vanaprastha Journey)
12. **The Guide (Sharing Wisdom)**: Mentoring younger generations.
13. **Detachment Practice**: Gradually reducing worldly entanglements.
14. **Grandparenting (Vatsalya)**: Spending quality time with grandchildren, passing on heritage.

## 🕉️ Phase 5: The Ultimate Peace (Sannyasa Journey)
15. **Total Focus (Moksha-Sadhana)**: Final quest for spiritual liberation.
16. **Universal Service**: Compassion for all living beings without personal attachment.
